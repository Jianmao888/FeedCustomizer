FeedCustomizer · Privacy Statement

FeedCustomizer respects your privacy. This statement explains how the app handles information and which third-party services may be involved when you use particular features.

Information collected

FeedCustomizer does not collect personal information and does not send information to servers operated by the developer.

The app requires a network connection only to:

  • download website images required by widget feeds; and
  • check the purchase or license status of the Donor Edition.

Microsoft Store and the Windows app host may collect usage information, such as app purchases, usage, or crash reports. If you open links to other websites or load content from them through the app, review the privacy policies of those websites.

Donor Edition

FeedCustomizer accesses the internet to check the purchase or license status of the Donor Edition. To reduce unnecessary requests, the app checks this status only on first launch.

Use of information

Except for the purposes above, FeedCustomizer does not collect or share other data online. We may revise this statement; changes will be published promptly in the updated statement.
